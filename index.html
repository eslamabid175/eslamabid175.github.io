About

Every product I work on has three parts: the Flutter app, the Python backend it talks to, and the pipeline that ships both. I work across all of them. Most of my work is cross-platform Flutter with Clean Architecture and BLoC, Django REST Framework on the server, and native Android or iOS code where Flutter needs it.

Since 2022 I've built a school platform used by parents, students, staff and bus drivers; community apps for the Muslim Association of Canada; AI-backed apps at 3I Vision; a trip platform for Oman; and my own shop-management product, Mksaby, which I built alone from the first table to the store listing.

I care about the parts users never see but always feel: offline-first data, fast media, background work that survives, and releases that don't break production. This year I finished a 30-lesson Senior / Lead roadmap to go deeper on exactly those things.

Mksaby مكسبي

Sales, purchases, stock and debts for small Egyptian shops, in Arabic, offline first.

Solo build. I designed, built, tested and published Mksaby entirely on my own, from the database schema to the store listing.

Google Play

Problem

Owners of clothing, shoe, mobile, electronics, wholesale and grocery shops need to see sales, stock, customer debts and real profit in one place: in Arabic, in Egyptian pounds, on their phone, and still working when the internet drops. Staff need to sell from their own phones without seeing everything.

What I built
  • Quick sales with barcode scanning and voice entry, PDF invoices shared over WhatsApp, and cash, wallet, InstaPay or credit payments.
  • Purchases, returns, stocktaking and inventory with weighted-average costing.
  • Customers and suppliers with debt statements (كشف حساب), expenses, a treasury ledger (الخزنة) and a gross versus net profit breakdown with charts.
  • Employees with granular permissions and PIN user switching, each logging in from their own phone and syncing live to the owner.
  • Arabic and English, dark theme, guest browsing, a 7-day free trial with operator-verified activation, and a forced-update gate.
Stack
Flutter, feature-first Clean Architecture, BLoC/Cubit, freezed, get_it + injectable, go_router, Drift (SQLite) with 22 synced tables, outbox sync to Cloud Firestore, Firebase phone OTP with WhatsApp OTP fallback, flutter_secure_storage, fpdart, fl_chart, mobile_scanner, Sentry, Patrol.
Outcome

Live on Google Play. Backed by around 229 unit and widget test files, Patrol end-to-end tests on real devices, and CI gates that check translation parity and design-token usage.

Offline first: 22 tables live in SQLite on the phone and sync to Firestore through an outbox, so a sale never waits for the network.

Aman

Trips for admins, drivers and passengers, with live tracking and wallet-aware payments.

Full product ownership. I built the Flutter app and the FastAPI services it runs on, from idea to launch.

Google PlayApp Store

Problem

A trip operator in Oman needed one platform for three roles: admins who plan trips, drivers who run them, and passengers who book and pay, with money handled by clear rules.

What I built
  • Trip templates, seat booking, live trip tracking, profiles and instant notifications.
  • Cash, gateway and hybrid payments with a wallet ledger and points, idempotent verification, and cancellation crediting rules. Booking state is kept separate from payment state.
  • Google Maps, geolocation and geocoding, driver live tracking, route workflows and multilingual UX.
  • FastAPI services with PostgreSQL and SQLAlchemy, Firebase Auth (Google, Apple, OTP) and role-based API design.
Stack
Flutter, flutter_bloc / hydrated_bloc, get_it, Dio, easy_localization, Google Maps, geolocator, Firebase Auth, FCM, flutter_secure_storage, Hive / SQLite; FastAPI, SQLAlchemy, Pydantic, PostgreSQL, Docker Compose, Gunicorn / Uvicorn, Nginx.
Outcome

Completed and published on Google Play and the App Store, deployed with Docker Compose behind Nginx and Makefile-based release workflows.
