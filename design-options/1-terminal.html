~/eslam-aped/README.md

I'm a software engineer who owns features end to end: the Flutter screens, the state and offline layer under them, the Django REST or FastAPI service they talk to, and the pipeline that ships them.

Since 2023 that has meant a school-management ecosystem and the Muslim Association of Canada apps at Cogens, AI-powered vision apps at 3I Vision, and freelance products like Mksaby, an offline-first shop accounting app I built alone from the first commit to the store listing.

I care about Clean Architecture, apps that keep working without a connection, and performance you can feel: fast uploads, smooth feeds, no jank. I recently finished a 30-lesson Senior / Lead roadmap covering system design, production DevOps and leading a team.

B.Sc. Computer Science, Cairo University. Speaks Arabic (native), English (fluent), French (intermediate).

freelance/mksaby

مكسبي, shop sales, purchases and inventory
  • live
  • solo

An Arabic-first (RTL) accounting and shop-management app for small Egyptian merchants: clothing, shoes, mobiles, electronics, wholesale and grocery. It works offline and syncs in real time across the owner's and employees' phones. I built it entirely myself, from architecture to store release.

  • Quick sales with barcode scanning, PDF invoices shared on WhatsApp, and cash, wallet, InstaPay or credit payments
  • Purchases, inventory with weighted-average costing, stocktaking and returns
  • Customer and supplier debt statements, expenses, a treasury ledger, and real gross-vs-net profit reports with charts
  • Employees with granular permissions and PIN user switching, logging in from their own phones and syncing live to the owner
  • 7-day free trial with operator-verified activation, forced-update gate, dark theme, Arabic and English

Under the hood: feature-first Clean Architecture, BLoC/Cubit, freezed, get_it + injectable, go_router, Drift (SQLite) with 22 synced tables and an offline outbox syncing to Cloud Firestore, Firebase phone OTP with WhatsApp OTP fallback, Sentry, Patrol on-device E2E tests, around 229 unit and widget test files, and CI gate scripts for ARB parity and theme tokens.

  • Flutter
  • Drift
  • Firestore sync
  • Offline-first
  • Clean Architecture
  • Patrol
