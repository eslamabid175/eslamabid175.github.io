Mksaby مكسبي

Sales, purchases and inventory for small Egyptian shops: clothing, shoes, mobiles, electronics, wholesale and grocery. Arabic-first (RTL), works offline, and syncs live across the owner’s and employees’ phones. I designed, built and shipped all of it myself.

What shop owners get
  • Quick sales with barcode scanning; invoice PDFs shared on WhatsApp
  • Cash, wallet, InstaPay and credit payments
  • Inventory with weighted-average costing, stocktaking and returns
  • Customer and supplier debt statements, expenses, treasury ledger
  • Real profit (gross vs net) and reports with charts
  • Employees with granular permissions and PIN switch-user
How it’s built
  • Feature-first Clean Architecture, BLoC/Cubit, freezed, get_it + injectable, go_router
  • Drift (SQLite) with 22 synced tables and an offline outbox to Cloud Firestore
  • Firebase phone OTP with a WhatsApp OTP fallback, secure storage
  • Sentry, Patrol on-device E2E tests, ~229 unit and widget test files
  • CI gates for ARB parity and theme-token usage; a design-token system
  • 7-day trial with operator-verified activation; forced-update gate
